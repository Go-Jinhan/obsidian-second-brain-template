# Where will you need this note next?

A note about reading could go with books, personal development, or your current reading experiment. Its subject alone does not tell you which place would be most useful.

*PARA* adds another question: **what are you going to use this information for?** Organizing around that answer saves you from gathering the same material again whenever you begin work.

## One subject, several roles

“Try a new reading-note method for four weeks” has an end and a completion condition. It belongs in [[_Projects.en]].

“Keep learning and reading regularly” continues after those four weeks. It involves standards and responsibilities to maintain, so it belongs in [[_Areas.en]].

“Methods for taking reading notes” is subject-based material you may consult at different times. Keep it in [[_Resources.en]]. When the experiment ends, put its inactive record in [[_Archives.en]].

Give each record a home according to its current role. Link to it from other places that need it to avoid maintaining unnecessary copies.

## Materials move through projects

At the start of a project, gather what you need from Capture, Resources, and earlier projects into its folder. Keep material you will use and develop close to the actual work.

When the project ends, move reusable knowledge and materials back to Resources. Preserve the project’s progress and results in Archives. Any ongoing standards or responsibilities can continue in Areas.

**These are places information can move between as its use changes.** [[_Projects.en]] shows the cycle in more detail.

## Start with the work you are doing

When reviewing Capture, first ask whether a note helps a current project. If it does, bring it into that context. Otherwise, consider an area you maintain or a subject you expect to revisit.

If the choice is unclear, imagine what you would be doing when you looked for the note. A recognizable title, a useful summary, and relevant links all help at that moment.

Material in Resources can become central to a new project. An old project can become a reference for the next one. Organization can be adjusted a little at a time as you use the notes.
