# Some things need care without having an end date

A presentation, a trip, or a four-week experiment can be completed. Health, relationships, learning, and professional responsibilities continue after an individual project ends.

*Areas* gives you a place to ask: **what do I want to keep looking after?**

## Keeping information and maintaining a responsibility

A collection of exercise articles belongs in *Resources*. Deciding what standards you want to maintain in your own life and reviewing whether you are maintaining them belongs in Areas.

For learning, you might record how you want to learn, what is difficult at present, and which learning projects you are working on. A few useful questions and links are enough to begin with.

## An area can lead to a project

While reviewing your intention to use what you read, you may decide the current method needs to change. Create a specific project: “Experiment with reading notes for four weeks.”

When it ends, return to the area and consider the result. Is there a habit worth maintaining? Has a new problem appeared? The area continues while individual projects begin and end.

## Why questions and ideas are here

This vault keeps [[_Feynmans-Questions.en]] and [[_Ideas.en]] under Areas as places for interests you intend to revisit over time.

This is a practical choice in this template; it does not mean every idea is a responsibility in *PARA*. Add other areas according to the responsibilities you actually maintain.

During the monthly review in [[_Periodic-Notes.en]], choose one important area. Consider whether it is receiving enough attention and whether a small project would help.
