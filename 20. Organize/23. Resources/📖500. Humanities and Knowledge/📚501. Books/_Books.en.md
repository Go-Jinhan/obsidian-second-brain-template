# Let the reading continue after you close the book

A book can seem full of useful material while you read it. Later, you may remember the cover and a few sentences but have no idea which chapter held the explanation you need. A book note leaves a route back.

You do not need to reproduce the whole book. **Keep enough to find why you read it, what stayed with you, and what you want to try.**

## The main note is an entry point

This vault uses a folder for each book and a main note named `📓B-Book Title`. In the introduction, write what prompted you to read it and a one-line account of what it offers.

The contents section helps you navigate the book. Link a separate chapter note when you need more detail. Every chapter does not need the same amount of summarizing.

## Distinguish a passage from your response

Use the quotations and personal reflections section for passages you want to remember. Mark direct quotations and include a chapter or page. Underneath, explain why the passage mattered to you.

After reading about a project box, for example, you might decide to write down your initial goal whenever you begin a project. That is a way for the book’s idea to enter your work.

## Connect ideas beyond the book

The key ideas and *Zettelkasten* section links chapter records and ideas developed from reading. A reference note explaining the book and a permanent note bringing together several sources or experiences have different roles.

Connect an action to a project. Develop an independent explanation in a permanent note. The book’s main note remains a way to find those connections.

The Book template includes reading status, start and finish dates, author, and other properties. Use them to track the reading while also considering what you understood and used.

Choose Book in the Basic template, enter a title and reading status, and begin with one question: **what am I hoping this book will help me understand?** See [[_Zettelkasten-ReferenceNotes.en]] for source notes and [[_Zettelkasten-PermanentNotes.en]] for ideas you develop.
