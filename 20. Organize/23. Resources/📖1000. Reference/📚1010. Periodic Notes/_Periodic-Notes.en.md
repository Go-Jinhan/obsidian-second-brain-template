# What changes when you return to your notes?

Recording each day is useful, but without looking back it can be hard to see what you learned or what keeps happening. Daily, weekly, and monthly notes give those records a recurring place in your attention.

Rereading can reveal something you missed. Add how your thinking changed and what you want to do next. The record then has a way to influence daily life.

## *Daily*: keep the experience

Use *Daily Journal* for events and thoughts, and *Completed / What I Learned* for accomplishments and learning. Weather, mood, and exercise can help you recall the day’s context.

You do not need to account for every hour. Keep the events or questions you want to revisit. If a thought continues to matter beyond the date, give it its own note and connect it.

## *Weekly*: notice what repeats

A minor delay on one day can become a pattern across a week. Look for work you keep postponing, habits that actually help, or tasks taking longer than expected.

The weekly template has room for daily highlights, lessons, gratitude, what worked, what needs adjusting, and plans for the following week. The useful question is **how this week’s experience should change next week’s actions**. Filling every section is optional.

Review *Capture* too. A forgotten question may now connect to your project. A few revisited notes can become useful materials for the next task.

## *Monthly*: review the direction as well as the work

Step back from individual tasks. Do your current projects serve the goals that matter? Is an area receiving too little attention? Is something repeatedly postponed still worth doing?

The Monthly template provides monthly highlights and a place to collect weekly summaries. Add questions about goals, projects, areas, and priorities as you need them. It does not automatically include every review question from the book.

Weekly reviews help with immediate adjustments; monthly reviews help with direction. Both can help you choose what deserves attention next.

Open this week’s record and name one thing to continue and one thing to change. Connect the answer to [[_Projects.en]] or [[_Areas.en]] so the reflection has somewhere to go. See the English README for plugin setup and template selection.
