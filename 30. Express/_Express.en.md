# What could you make with what you already have?

Reading, saving, and organizing can feel productive. But a long preparation phase can also keep the actual work waiting. *Express* gives you a reason to use the material now.

Explain something to another person, make a small result, or try to solve a problem you face. You can begin before collecting every possible source.

## Sit down with your materials

Suppose you want to write a short article but cannot find a starting point. Open a few relevant reference and permanent notes. You may already have an idea to explain, evidence, and an example. Arrange them and begin writing the connections.

The *Archipelago of Ideas* in *Building a Second Brain* offers this approach: connect islands of existing thought and work into a larger whole.

A first result can be small—a paragraph, a small feature, or one experiment. These intermediate pieces can become material for other work later.

## Why are permanent notes here?

This vault places [[_Zettelkasten-PermanentNotes.en]] under Express because explaining an idea in your own words goes beyond recording a source.

A permanent note is still a reusable unit of thought. It does not need to be a finished talk or published article. The final result may live in a project folder or another tool. Express describes the act of using knowledge as well as the role of this part of the vault.

## Use brings up the next question

Explaining something may reveal a gap in the argument. Applying it may produce a result you did not expect. Put the new question in *Capture*, revisit the evidence, and improve the note.

Choose two or three related notes. What is the smallest useful thing you could make from them? Use [[_Projects.en]] to organize the work and leave *The Hemingway Bridge* when you need to pause.
