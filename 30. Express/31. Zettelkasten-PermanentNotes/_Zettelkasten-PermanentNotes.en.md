# Give your own understanding a clear form

You may be able to summarize a source and still hesitate when asked what you think about it. Its relationship to your experience and other knowledge may need more work.

A *Permanent Note* is a place for that work. **Explain one idea, show why you understand it that way, and connect it to other ideas.**

## Let the title convey the idea

“The Hemingway Bridge” names a concept. “Leaving a next action makes it easier to restart work” expresses an idea you can examine through using it.

In the body, explain what you mean, what supports it, and when you would need to reconsider it. Aim for an explanation someone can follow without knowing the original source:

> Recording a specific next action before stopping may reduce the need to decide where to begin when returning to the task.
>
> If the situation has changed, check whether that action is still appropriate.

This is an illustrative example. In your own note, connect the relevant sources and experiences so the reasoning can be examined.

## Keep the idea small enough to reuse

When several arguments occupy one note, it is difficult to use just the part you need. A clearly stated idea is easier to connect to another piece of writing or a project.

Explain the relationship when linking another note: it supports the claim, offers a counterexample, or records an application. The explanation makes the connection useful to your thinking.

## Permanent does not mean unchangeable

A first explanation may be incomplete. Revise it after reading more or trying the idea yourself. Returning to notes, connecting them, and developing them is part of the practice.

A question in *Fleeting Notes* and evidence in *Reference Notes* may lead to an explanation that belongs here. You do not need to turn every source into a permanent note or simply rename it.

[[_Distill.en]] explains the essentials of an existing note. A permanent note holds an idea you can use in more than one context.

Choose one question and write what you currently understand and why. [[_Express.en]] takes that thinking into actual use.
