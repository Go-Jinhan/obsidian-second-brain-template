## Core idea

Leaving a specific next action may reduce the need to decide where to begin when returning to a task.

## Reasoning and evidence

This illustrative note develops the concept in [[Example - The Hemingway Bridge.en|The Hemingway Bridge]] in different words. It is not an independent experimental result or a claim of a universal effect.

## Related ideas

The question in [[Example - Leave a Next Action.en|Leave a next action]] can be explored in [[📦P-Example Reading Experiment.en|Example Reading Experiment]].

## Applications and limits

Before stopping, write one next action and the context needed to carry it out. If the situation changes, review the earlier plan before following it.
