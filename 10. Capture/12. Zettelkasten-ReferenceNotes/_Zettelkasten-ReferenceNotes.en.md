# Keep the reason you saved the link

You bookmark a useful article. Later, its contents look familiar, but you cannot remember why you saved it. Finding the important part means reading it all over again.

A *Reference Note* preserves your understanding at the time: **where you found the material, what you understood, and why it mattered to you**. Books, lectures, videos, articles, and technical documentation can all be recorded this way.

## Put your understanding beside the source

For an article about reading, you might write:

> Main point: explaining something in your own words after reading can reveal gaps in understanding.
>
> My question: would this help when reviewing work documents?
>
> Somewhere to try it: give a short explanation of the main point at the next document review.

Writing in your own words helps you see what you understand and what you still need to check. If you copy a sentence, mark it as a quotation and keep enough detail to find it again: author, URL, chapter, or page.

Separate the source’s claims from your own guesses and proposed applications. That makes it easier to revise your thinking without losing its basis.

## Make the useful part easy to find

Once you understand the material, put its essentials in *Distill* at the top. Keep the fuller explanation and context below. A quick visit can begin with the summary; a careful check can continue into the body and original source.

The Reference option in the Basic template includes a `source` property. Fill it in even if the source also appears in the body. Use a URL, bibliographic details and a chapter or page, or a link to another note.

## Take the material where you will use it

This folder is an entry point. If you will use and develop a note in a current project, bring it into that project’s folder. Put material you want to find by subject in [[_Resources.en]]. Its Reference type and source remain useful wherever it lives.

For a whole book’s reading record, see [[_Books.en]]. For an idea you want to explain independently, see [[_Zettelkasten-PermanentNotes.en]]. Keep a link to the reference that helped you get there.
