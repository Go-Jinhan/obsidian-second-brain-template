[한국어](<Example - Leave a Next Action.md>) · **English**

> [!abstract] Distill
> - Would leaving a next action before stopping make it easier to restart?

Fictional observation: after stopping in the middle of a reading note, I spent time the next day trying to remember what I had intended to do.

Next time I stop, I will leave one action I can begin with immediately.
