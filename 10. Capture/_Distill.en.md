# Make a long note worth opening again

You find the note you need, but it fills the screen with text. You have read and organized this before; now you must work out what mattered all over again. When time is short, it can feel easier to search elsewhere.

*Distill* helps at that moment. **It gives your future self enough understanding to decide whether this note is useful.**

## Summarize for the next reading

*Progressive Summarization* works through layers: mark important passages, highlight the most useful parts, and extract a short account of what matters. The purpose is to make the material easier to find and use again.

For a long note about *The Hemingway Bridge*, the top might say:

> Before stopping work, record the next action and unresolved questions. These cues can help restore the context when you return.
>
> In this project, update The Hemingway Bridge at the end of each work session.

That is enough to recognize the idea and a possible use. The detailed method and its conditions remain in the body.

## Why keep the summary at the top?

The summary helps you read this particular note. Keeping it at the beginning lets you move from a quick overview directly into the evidence and explanation below.

When the note moves to *Projects* or *Resources*, its summary travels with it. You do not need to visit another folder to work out what the summary refers to. This is why the Basic template includes Distill for Idea and Reference notes, rather than creating a separate Distill folder.

## What should you keep?

Look away from the body and ask what you would want to remember next time. A concept, conclusion, or possible application is a useful starting point. Include an important limitation if the claim depends on particular conditions.

You can leave Distill empty while capturing and fill it in after you understand the material. Revise it as you use the note. Keep the essentials without writing the whole note a second time.

**Distill summarizes the note it belongs to. A permanent note explains an idea in its own right.** When your understanding becomes an independent argument, continue in [[_Zettelkasten-PermanentNotes.en]].
